Everyone is Welcome

We warmly welcome patients from all across Melbourne and beyond. No matter where you're coming from, our doors are open — we'd love to be your dental home.

Getting Here

Easy to find, easy to park

By Car

There is a small shared parking lot directly in front of the building, shared with the pharmacy and medical clinic next door. Additional free on-street parking is also available on Kings Rd. We're easily accessible from the Western Ring Road and Deer Park Bypass.

By Bus

Several bus routes service Kings Rd. Check Public Transport Victoria (PTV) for the most up-to-date timetables and route information.

Hours Reminder

Open Monday to Saturday. We recommend calling ahead to confirm your appointment time. Walk-ins are welcome where availability allows.
