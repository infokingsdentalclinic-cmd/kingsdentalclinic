Our Story

About Us

A welcoming dental clinic built on a simple belief — that great dental care should be accessible, transparent, and genuinely comfortable for every patient and their families.

Established 2007

Who We Are

Trusted care for the whole family

Kings Dental Clinic is a private practice located in the heart of Kings Park, Victoria. We warmly welcome patients from all across Melbourne — whether you're just around the corner or travelling from further afield, you'll always find a friendly face and exceptional care here.

Operating in the community since 2007, Kings Dental Clinic was built by family, for families — a clinic where locals could always find trusted, welcoming care close to home. That same spirit still shapes how we care for every patient today, whether you've been with us for years or you're visiting for the first time.

Our clinic was founded with the goal of creating a dental practice that patients genuinely look forward to visiting — one where the focus is always on long-term health, honest communication, and a truly personalised experience.

We take the time to understand each patient's needs and goals, and we explain every treatment option clearly so you always feel confident and informed about your care.

What We Stand For

Our values

Patient-First Care

Every decision we make starts with what's best for you. We take the time to ease any anxiety, listen to your concerns, and explain your options clearly — so you always feel like a valued member of our family, not just a patient.

Transparency

No hidden fees, no unnecessary upselling. We provide clear treatment plans with written fee estimates before we begin, so you always know what to expect.

Clinical Excellence

We stay current with the latest techniques and technology in dentistry to ensure you receive the highest standard of evidence-based care available.
